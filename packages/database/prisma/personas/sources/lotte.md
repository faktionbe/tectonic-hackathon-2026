## Gaps and risks KBC can spot

1. **She has no fire insurance.** Since 2019, tenants in Flanders are legally required to have it. This is a genuine customer need, not just an upsell.
2. **Her address is outdated.** Official letters and cards may go to Kortrijk.
3. **Her savings are down about €3,000.** She may want a "rebuild my buffer" goal.
4. **Costs are shared with Thomas through manual transfers.** A joint account or shared budget could help.
5. **She no longer has commuting costs.** She's saving about €95 a month that could be redirected.
6. **Great understanding of financial knowledge**
